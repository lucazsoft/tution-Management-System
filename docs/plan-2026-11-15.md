## Phase 6: App stores (2–3 days of work, plus waiting)

> Start the accounts and D-U-N-S number **now**; they can run alongside phases 2–5.

- [ ] Decide whose name the store accounts are in: the client's (organization) or ours.
- [ ] **Google Play** ($25). A new *personal* account needs a closed test with 12 testers for 14 days before going public. An *organization* account skips that but needs a D-U-N-S number.
- [ ] **Apple Developer** ($99/year). An organization account needs a D-U-N-S number (1–2 weeks). A Mac or cloud Mac is needed for builds.
- [ ] Store listing: icon, screenshots, description, **privacy policy URL**, Play Data safety form, Apple privacy labels.
- [ ] Signing keys, stored safely. Release builds pointed at the HTTPS production API (`API_BASE_URL` without `/api`).
- [ ] Submit. Apple review takes 1–3 days; a rejection adds a round.
